the fine print, but cute

Before you book

Join Hilton Honors first

The prices above are the Hilton Sale rate, about 20% off. It needs a free Hilton Honors account, so sign up before you book. You can still cancel up to 14 days before check-in.

Without an account, the regular flexible rate was $654 a night (about $1,525 per room, or $763 each).

What's in the price

  • The $45 nightly resort charge
  • Taxes and fees: 16% plus $5 a night
  • Not parking or meals

Hilton's checkout page shows your exact total before you pay.

The resort charge gets you

  • 2 signature cocktails every evening at The Pocket Cafe
  • Library access and stargazing gear
  • Bikes, tennis and pickleball, Wi-Fi
